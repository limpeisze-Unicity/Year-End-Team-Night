02. Preparation Guide

Dress Code & What to Bring

Three practical notes so you can arrive comfortable, prepared, and ready to enjoy the rooftop evening.

Official Attire Theme
Smart casual, with a touch of gold
Subtle metallic accents encouraged · Full formalwear not required
Tip 01 · Attire

Style the Touch of Gold

Pair relaxed tailored separates, a sharp linen shirt, or a midi dress with one warm metallic detail—think a gold watch, cufflinks, silk scarf, statement earrings, or gold-trimmed loafers.

Comfort comes first; smart dark denim and clean dress sneakers are welcome.
Tip 02 · Rooftop Comfort

Bring an Evening Layer

While the Level 12 dining pergola is sheltered and equipped with overhead radiant heaters, the open observation deck can catch a breeze after 8:30 pm. Pack a tailored blazer, light knit, or wrap.

Timber deck flooring throughout—block heels, wedges, or flats are ideal.
Tip 03 · Essentials

Staff Pass & Safe Ride Plan

Bring your building access badge for express elevator entry to Level 12 after 6:00 pm, and make sure the Uber app is installed on your phone to redeem your complimentary $35 ride-home voucher.

A staffed cloakroom is available right by the Level 12 lift lobby for laptops and work bags.
